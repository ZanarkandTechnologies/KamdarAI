# Kamdar manager live proof

## Audience and claim

Audience: Vishan and a technical collaborator evaluating whether the proposed
manager workflow is concrete enough to continue.

Claim: one namespaced, bounded live POC selected the right evidence, produced
area reports with project subsections and a company rollup, made the expected
Notion and Drive writes, sent exactly two allowlisted emails and one Telegram
summary, and scored the recorded receipts without activating production.

1. Before: the workflow existed as prose and fragmented mock cases.
2. After: one inspectable Test / Run / Results proof covers the full path.
3. Example: Live POC mode records 13 calls and passes 37 of 37 assertions.
4. Output: the standalone showcase exposes reports, outreach, and evidence.
5. Boundary: production scheduling remains proposal-only.
